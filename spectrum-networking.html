NVIDIA Spectrum Networking

Configure, optimize, secure, and troubleshoot Spectrum-X switches. Demonstrate skills using tools such as NVIDIA NetQ, CloudAI Benchmark, and DPUs; define advanced QoS and telemetry; monitor and tune network performance; and resolve complex real-world connectivity and microsegmentation scenarios in Ethernet-based AI fabrics.

Exam objectives

  1. 2.1Configure NVIDIA Spectrum-X switches for RDMA over Converged Ethernet (RoCE) to enable high-speed, low-latency communication.
  2. 2.2Enable and verify QoS, explicit congestion notification (ECN), and priority flow control (PFC), advanced features like adaptive routing, and telemetry.
  3. 2.3Configure multi-tenancy Border Gateway Protocol Ethernet VPN (BGP-EVPN) to isolate tenant workloads.
  4. 2.4Use NVIDIA Air to simulate network environments and identify potential issues.
  5. 2.5Diagnose congestion or packet loss using in-band telemetry and NVIDIA What Just Happened (WJH) services.
  6. 2.6Use NetQ for real-time network monitoring, including congestion detection and latency measurements.
  7. 2.7Install NVIDIA DOCA.
  8. 2.8Configure NVIDIA SuperNIC functionality for advanced packet processing and congestion control.
