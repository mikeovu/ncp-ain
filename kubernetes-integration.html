Kubernetes Integration

Deploy and manage the NVIDIA Network Operator and device plug-ins to enable RDMA/InfiniBand within Kubernetes clusters, validate GPU resources, and ensure seamless integration with AI workloads. Includes troubleshooting cluster-network interfaces, deploying drivers, and scheduling resources on heterogeneous infrastructure.
